I am excited to share that I attended the 9th Asian Quantitative Finance Conference held from April 26-28, 2025 in Shenzhen, China. It was a fantastic opportunity to connect with fellow researchers and professionals in the field of quantitative finance.

---

I was honored to present my recent work on "SOFR Futures Pricing under Affine Term Structure Models with Scheduled Jumps" during the conference. The feedback and discussions were incredibly valuable, and I am grateful for the insights shared by the attendees.
